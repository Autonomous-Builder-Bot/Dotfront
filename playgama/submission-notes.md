Short description:

Full description:
Build a stellar empire one planet at a time in Dotfront: Swarm Command. Four units have distinct roles: fast circles for swarming, armored squares for defense, ranged laser triangles, and heavy rectangles for combat. Select your forces with intuitive touch controls, capture neutral worlds, set rally points, upgrade factories and earn permanent upgrades. Learn through small guided skirmishes, then take on increasingly difficult AI opponents across a full campaign.

How to play:
Tap one blue unit or drag a box around several blue units. Tap a destination to move or a red/gray planet to attack. Tap a blue planet to change its production and set a rally point. Send 20 dots close to a friendly planet and press Upgrade to improve it. Circles are fast, squares are armored, triangles attack from range, and rectangles fight units but cannot capture planets. Win by capturing all red planets.
