# Dotfront — Historical Campaign, Levels 1–100

## Design principles

These 100 missions are **tactical adaptations inspired by historical battles**, not literal maps of historical terrain. The player's existing movement and production controls are unchanged; the experience comes from differences in planet spacing, ownership, reinforcement distances, AI doctrine and starting strength.

The first 20 levels **preserve all existing handcrafted tutorial layouts**. Levels 21–100 use deterministic authored formation blueprints, with an individualized layout and tactical lesson for every battle. Higher levels gradually increase world size, planet count and the baseline starting armies. Large chapter finales add an additional enemy starting force.

Every battle still uses the standard win condition: capture all enemy-owned planets. Rectangles continue to require capturing infantry support. The opening set uses five existing AI strategic behaviors; no terrain walls, scripted waves or hidden movement restrictions have been added.

**Not guaranteed:** Equal strategic difficulty, historical fidelity, uniqueness as perceived by every player or optimal balancing for all purchased upgrades. The design was audited for deterministic geometry and basic validity, not play-tested.

## Five connected chapters

### I — First Contact (levels 1–20)

Basic production, expansion, route choice and the early tutorial. Keep the existing authored tutorial geometry and overlay historical titles and tactical takeaways.

### II — Maneuver (levels 21–40)

Staggered formations, feints, flanking wings, relays, local superiority and first large sieges. Cavalry becomes available.

### III — Encirclement (levels 41–60)

Enclosure, opposing fronts, forward outposts, double-ring siege problems, and coordinated counterattack.

### IV — Reinforcement and Relief (levels 61–80)

Separated forces, rescue operations, three-lane warfare, resilient defenses and multiple hostile staging positions.

### V — Operational Command (levels 81–100)

Complex multi-pronged fronts, columns, reserve forces, fortified cores, and the fleet-sized battle of Sluys.

## Implementation notes

- `historical_missions.js` holds the 100 battle titles and briefings.
- `app.js` contains the formation blueprints and deterministic layout generator.
- `index.html` loads the mission data before the game engine.
- `sw.js` caches the new dataset for offline play.
- Existing campaign save keys and upgrade IDs are retained.
- Levels 101–250 still use the existing mathematical generator and are available for later historic adaptation.
